# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 8, 2026

This application ("the App") is a personal-use tool that converts Amazon product links into Amazon Associates affiliate links and publishes them as Pins to Pinterest on behalf of a single Pinterest account owned by the developer.

## Who this applies to

The App is used only by its developer, for their own Pinterest account. It is not distributed to, or used on behalf of, any other person or business.

## What data the App accesses

- **Pinterest data:** The App accesses only the authenticated account's own boards and Pins, via the Pinterest API, in order to create and manage Pins. It does not access data belonging to any other Pinterest user.
- **Amazon data:** The App reads publicly available Amazon product URLs supplied by the developer (e.g., product page links) to generate affiliate links. It does not access any Amazon account data.

## What data the App does not collect

The App does not collect, store, or share personal information about Pinterest users, Pinners, or any third party. It does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking of any kind. No data is sold or shared with any third party.

## Data storage

Any credentials (such as API tokens) used by the App are stored locally in an environment file on the developer's own machine and are not transmitted anywhere other than to the Pinterest and Amazon APIs required for the App to function.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the App's functionality changes. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
